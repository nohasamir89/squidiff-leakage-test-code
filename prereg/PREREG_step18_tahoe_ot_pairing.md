# Pre-registration — Step 18: optimal-transport pairing of the training controls (roadmap item 12) on the Tahoe brain/gut lines

Date: 2026-10-09. Written before any Step 18 checkpoint exists. Inputs: the Step 15a data (`tahoe_results/step15_data_record.json`),
the Step 15b recipe checkpoints and frozen arrays, and the OT-paired control files written by `check_ot_pairing_tahoe.py`
(commit b0f11bd; record `tahoe_results/ot_pairing_check.json`, commit a133ea3).

## 1. Question

The recipe is conditioned on one vehicle cell per treated cell (Change B); in training that vehicle cell is a random
cell of the same line and plate, so it carries the line and plate and nothing about the treated cell. Item 12 asks:
if each treated cell is instead paired with the vehicle cell closest to it — the exact minimum-cost matching, i.e.
discrete optimal transport between the two equal-weight empirical measures of a condition — does the model produce
cell populations closer to the real treated populations? The item-12 check measured what such a pairing carries:
the matched vehicle shares a centred cosine of 0.37 with its treated cell (random pairing 0.00; 5–95 % 0.31–0.42 over
11,248–11,510 training conditions per split), and the pair distance falls from 11.79 to 9.46, still above the distance
to the control mean (8.39). A moderate signal; whether the model can use it is the question. Step 6's arm C
(flow matching from the control cell with minibatch OT coupling, sci-Plex) was not adopted; it changed the objective,
this changes only the data pairing.

- **Q1 (primary, gated):** energy distance between the predicted cells and the real treated cells (the Step 15b
  cell-level metric), `ot_pair` − recipe, per split. Below zero = better.
- **Q2 (gate):** DE-overlap, `ot_pair` − recipe, per split: must not be below zero.
- **Q3 (gate):** PDS (Step 15c), `ot_pair` − recipe, per split: must not be below zero.

## 2. Arm and comparator

- **ot_pair**: the Step 15b recipe — adaLN, Gaussian-source flow matching, fingerprint drug encoding (1,024 bits),
  mlp encoder, cond_drop 0, 100,000 steps, EMA, the same code path and arguments as the Step 15b recipe runs — trained
  with `--control_data_path` pointing at `tahoe_train_<split>_control_ot.h5ad` instead of
  `tahoe_train_<split>_control.h5ad`. The OT file holds exactly the rows of the original control file (same cells, same
  multiset), permuted within each condition so that row *i* is the vehicle cell matched to treated row *i*; sha256
  pinned in `tahoe_results/ot_pairing_check.json` (`written`) and asserted by the launcher. Nothing else changes:
  same treated cells, same test files, same marginal distribution of the conditioning input, same sampler. Parameter
  count asserted equal to the recipe's (58,178,180). Seeds 0–2 on each of the three splits: 9 training runs.
- **Comparator**: the Step 15b recipe (nine checkpoints under `step15/recipe/`, frozen arrays
  `tahoe_results/vectors_s15_<split>_recipe_seed_<k>.npz` and `_pc.npz`), scored exactly as in Steps 15b, 15c and 16.
- The pairing change is the whole arm; the control for "any retraining" is the recipe's own three seeds, as in Step 16.

## 3. Evaluation

`leakage_test_fm_step18.py`, generated by `port_harness_step18.py` from `leakage_test_fm_tahoe.py`: the Step 15
harness with one addition — the per-cell file also stores the control cells each generated cell was conditioned on
(`ctrl`, the harness's own Protocol-B draw: `RandomState(i_cond)` over the (line, plate) vehicle pool). Output only:
no model call, draw or selection changes. 50 NFE Euler, unguided, 200-cell cap, Protocol B with random vehicle
cells of the condition's line and plate, as every Tahoe evaluation. Eighteen evaluations: the nine `ot_pair`
checkpoints and the nine recipe checkpoints re-evaluated under the new harness. Guard on the recipe re-evaluations:
`cond`, `true` and `C` identical to the Step 15b arrays, `B` and the per-cell `pred` within 1e-3 (max-abs) of them
— the harness change is output-only — and the Step 15b arrays remain the recipe's scored arrays.

## 4. Endpoints

Per split, 97.5 % structure-clustered percentile bootstrap intervals (5,000 replicates, `RandomState(0)`), per-condition
metrics fixed under resampling, MRA and the descriptor of 4.4 recomputed per replicate; arms per seed, then averaged
per condition, as Step 16. Every Step 15b, 15c and 16 value used is re-derived and asserted to 1e-9 before any Step 18
number is printed.

### 4.1 Gated: Q1 energy, Q2 DE-overlap, Q3 PDS (`ot_pair` − recipe)

### 4.2 Secondary: DPC-50, MRA (42 contexts), cross-plate PDS (Step 15c §8 facet), each `ot_pair` − recipe; worst seed
and worst split per metric; absolute values per seed.

### 4.3 Secondary: `ot_pair` − 0.5 on PDS and `ot_pair` − 0 on MRA (the arm's own signal), per split.

### 4.4 Exploratory: control tracking (does the generated cell follow the cell it was conditioned on?)

Per condition, the mean over its cells of the cosine between (generated cell − mean of the generated cells) and (its
conditioning control cell − mean of the conditioning control cells); per seed, then averaged; mean over conditions.
With random training pairs the model has no reason to track the control cell, so the recipe should sit near zero — a
positive value for the recipe would mean it partly copies its input (the control-paste tendency of Step 15b). The
contrast `ot_pair` − recipe (drug-clustered interval) says whether the OT pairing was learned at all. Not gated; it
explains a null (pairing not used) or a gain (pairing used), it does not decide the category.

### 4.5 Exploratory: learnability (Step 15b descriptor) against the per-condition energy gain of `ot_pair` over the recipe.

## 5. Verdict rule (coded in the scorer, precedence 1 → 2 → 5 → 3 → 4)

1. **FIDELITY GAIN** — Q1 below zero on all three splits; Q2 and Q3 not below zero on any split.
2. **FIDELITY GAIN AT A COST** — Q1 below zero on all three splits; Q2 or Q3 below zero on at least one split.
5. **UNRESOLVED** — Q1 includes zero on all three splits.
3. **REVERSED** — Q1 above zero on any split.
4. **PARTIAL** — otherwise (Q1 below zero on one or two splits, above zero on none).

"Includes zero" is "unresolved", never "equivalent".

## 6. Splits and discipline

Development: `drug_split_1` (three seeds), evaluated and scored alone (`_development` records) before any confirmation
checkpoint exists. Confirmation: `drug_split_0` and `drug_split_2`, six runs, then the final scoring of all three
splits. Nothing in the scorer changes between the two runs; any addition made after the development read is dated into
§4 as a pre-run paragraph before the confirmation runs, never a new section. The scorer refuses to run uncommitted.
The same splits as Steps 15b–16 (fourth use); no selection among arms — there is one arm.

## 7. Expectation (written before the run)

Category **5, UNRESOLVED**: energy `ot_pair` − recipe within ± 0.005 on every split (recipe 0.2250 / 0.2438 / 0.2181),
DE-overlap within ± 0.005, PDS within ± 0.01, MRA within ± 0.03. Control tracking: recipe between 0 and +0.1;
`ot_pair` above the recipe by +0.05 to +0.2 if the pairing was learned — the reading I expect is "learned, but no
fidelity gain", because a single matched vehicle cell is noisier than the control mean the model already sees
through the line and plate. Category 4 (energy gain on the development split only) is the next most likely.

## 8. Limits

One arm, no second control beyond the recipe's own seeds, so a resolved gain of the size of between-training-batch
variation (Step 13b) would need a reseed control before it is claimed; the pairing is Euclidean on the 200-gene
log-normalised panel, one of several possible costs; 150 training cells per condition bound how well a matching can
be; the test-time conditioning cells are random vehicle cells, as in training for the recipe but not for `ot_pair`
(whose training pairs were matched) — a train/test mismatch that is part of what is being tested, not a confound to
remove, because at test time no treated cell exists to match against; the descriptor of 4.4 is exploratory; three
splits used four times.
